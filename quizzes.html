Weekly Quizzes


Quiz Information

  • Weekly quizzes are administered every Wednesday at the beginning of class
  • Each quiz is worth 10 points
  • Quizzes are automatically graded through Canvas
  • Quiz format: True/False and Multiple-Choice questions
  • Content: Based on side topics and material presented during the week
  • Time limit: 15 minutes from the start of class
  • Total of 14 quizzes throughout the semester

Quiz Schedule

Quizzes will be available on Canvas every Wednesday. Please ensure you arrive on time to class to have the full 15 minutes to complete each quiz.

Make-up Quiz Policy

  • Make-up quizzes are available during office hours
  • Must have a legitimate excuse (doctor's note, family emergency, etc.)
  • Must receive approval from the instructor or Lead-TA
  • Contact the teaching team as soon as possible if you need to arrange a make-up quiz

Tips for Success

  • Review lecture notes and side topics presented during the week
  • Attend all classes to stay current with the material
  • Take notes during lectures
  • Ask questions during class or office hours if you need clarification

Access quizzes through Canvas at the beginning of each Wednesday class session.
