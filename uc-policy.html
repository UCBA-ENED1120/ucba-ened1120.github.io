University Policies


Academic Integrity

The University of Cincinnati is committed to academic integrity and expects all students to adhere to the highest standards of honesty in their academic work. Students are expected to complete their own work and properly cite all sources. Violations of academic integrity include, but are not limited to:

  • Plagiarism
  • Cheating on exams or assignments
  • Unauthorized collaboration
  • Falsification of data or records

For more information, visit the UC Academic Integrity Policy.

Disability Services

The University of Cincinnati is committed to providing reasonable accommodations to students with disabilities. Students with disabilities who need accommodations should contact:

Accessibility Resources
Phone: (513) 556-6823
Email: accessibility@uc.edu
Website: UC Accessibility Resources

Students should contact Accessibility Resources as early as possible in the semester to ensure timely provision of accommodations.

Title IX and Non-Discrimination

The University of Cincinnati does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national origin, sex, disability, religion, age, sexual orientation, gender identity, pregnancy, genetic information, or military status. The university is committed to providing a safe and inclusive environment for all students.

If you have experienced discrimination or harassment, please contact:

Office of Gender Equity and Inclusion
Phone: (513) 556-3349
Website: UC Equity and Inclusion

Student Code of Conduct

All students are expected to adhere to the University of Cincinnati Student Code of Conduct. The Code of Conduct outlines expectations for student behavior and the processes for addressing violations.

For more information, visit the UC Student Code of Conduct.

Mental Health and Wellness

The University of Cincinnati is committed to supporting the mental health and wellness of all students. If you are experiencing stress, anxiety, depression, or other mental health concerns, please reach out for support:

Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS)
Phone: (513) 556-0648
Website: UC CAPS

CAPS provides free, confidential counseling services to all UC students.

Emergency Procedures

In the event of an emergency, please follow the instructions of university officials and emergency personnel. Familiarize yourself with emergency exits and procedures for your classroom and building.

For more information on emergency procedures and safety, visit UC Public Safety.

Attendance and Participation

Regular attendance and active participation are essential for success in this course. Students are expected to attend all class sessions and complete all assigned work. If you must miss class due to illness or other legitimate reasons, please notify the instructor as soon as possible.

For complete university policies, please visit the UC Policies website.
